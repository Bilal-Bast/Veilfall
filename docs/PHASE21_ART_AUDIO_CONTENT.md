# Phase 21 — Regional Art, Audio, and Scribe Follow-up

This phase adds the first integrated replacement for the prototype's visual placeholders and a more complete Refuge side-story reward.

## Included

- Six illustrated 960×540 region backdrops: Whispering Woods, Lantern Refuge, Ashen Foundry, Drowned Crypt, Veil Citadel, and Echo Depths.
- A new pixel-art Kael idle sprite with a transparent background. Movement bob, hit flashes, sword arcs, and Lantern Pulse effects remain driven by the existing player script.
- Region-specific platform palettes for the Woods, Foundry, Crypt, Citadel, and Echo Depths, including matching spectral platform colors.
- Six original looping regional music beds and six ambience beds, generated by `tools/build_phase21_assets.py` and included as WAV files.
- A region audio autoload that follows the player's room, crossfades music and ambience, stays quiet on the title screen, and stops when gameplay exits.
- The Scribe's follow-up story: after restoring the three folios and defeating the Echo Warden, return to the Refuge to claim the Resonant Wick. It reduces Lantern Pulse cooldown and persists through the existing collected-item save list.

## Focused playtest

1. Launch from the title screen and confirm no gameplay soundtrack plays before entering a journey.
2. Start or continue. Confirm the Woods music and ambience begin with a short fade.
3. Walk from Woods to Refuge, Foundry, Crypt, Citadel, and Echo Depths; confirm each area's music and ambience change once, without restarting for every room within one region.
4. Switch realms and confirm the backdrop tint and spectral platforms still update.
5. Check Kael faces both directions, bobs while moving, flashes on hit, and still shows the sword and pulse effects.
6. Confirm platforms use distinct regional palettes, especially in spectral form.
7. Restore all three archive folios, defeat the Echo Warden, return to the Scribe, and claim the Resonant Wick. Reopen the game and confirm the item persists and Lantern Pulse has the shorter cooldown.
8. Return to the title screen and confirm regional audio stops.

## Current art boundary

This is an integrated art foundation, not a complete final art pack. Kael currently has an idle frame rather than a full animation sheet. Enemy and boss sprites, regional foreground tile atlases, parallax layers, and final NPC portraits still need a dedicated art pass. The region backdrops are concept-art style paintings and should be checked in Godot at the intended camera crop before final release.

## Asset provenance

The six backdrops and Kael sprite were generated specifically for Veilfall. Music and ambience loops are original synthesized assets generated by the included Python tool. The tool's Python dependencies (Pillow, NumPy, SciPy) are only needed to rebuild the generated assets; Godot runs the included files without Python dependencies.

## Validation

Run `python3 tools/verify_project.py` from the project root. Godot runtime validation is still required for visual crop, GDScript parsing, audio transitions, and the new Scribe reward.
